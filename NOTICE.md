# Notices and data policy

This repository contains only original research code, aggregate experimental
results, documentation, and an artwork-free visualization rendered from an
episode replay.

It does not distribute:

- card artwork, card metadata exports, or competition PDFs;
- competition simulator binaries or shared libraries;
- raw episode replays or participant identifiers;
- trained competition model weights;
- Kaggle credentials, access tokens, cookies, or local configuration;
- packaged competition submissions.

Users who participate in a competition are responsible for obtaining its data
and runtime through authorized channels and complying with the applicable
competition rules.

Pokémon and related names are trademarks of their respective owners. Their use
here identifies the research setting and does not imply affiliation.
